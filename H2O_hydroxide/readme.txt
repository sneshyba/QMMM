In the folder 1_H2O_no_optimization

The notebook "fixed-water calculation.ipymb" is an exploration of a single QM hydroxide molecule interacting with a single MM water (details in the header). It carries out a "QM/MM electrostatic embedding," meaning the properties of the MM water -- including LJ interactions and electrostatic interactions -- are included in the QM hamiltonian of the hydroxide.

The notebook "scanned water.ipynb" does the same thing, but over a range of positions.
